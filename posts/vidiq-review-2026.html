What VidIQ Is Used For

VidIQ is a YouTube growth toolkit: keyword research, competitor tracking, thumbnail A/B testing, and a "scorecard" that grades your video before publish. The pitch is turning guesswork into a repeatable upload process — findable topics, better titles, and coaching that explains why a video underperforms.

How We Tested It

We used VidIQ to pick three keywords, compared its search-volume and competition scores against YouTube's own suggestions, then published with its title and tag recommendations. We tracked impressions and click-through over four weeks.

Pros and Cons

  • Pros: clear keyword metrics, browser extension shows data on any video, coaching prompts are actionable, free tier is usable.
  • Cons: some "growth score" weightings feel opaque; daily limits on lower tiers; coaching repeats generic advice at times.

Pricing

VidIQ has a free plan and several paid tiers that unlock deeper research, more daily actions, and advanced coaching. Check the site for current rates; we mark them approximate when they change.

Where It Actually Helps

The keyword tool is the real value: it surfaces search demand you'd miss by intuition alone. The scorecard stops you publishing a title no one clicks. Neither replaces good content, but both raise the floor on every upload — which compounds over dozens of videos.

Bottom Line

VidIQ is worth it for creators publishing regularly who want data, not vibes. Free tier first; upgrade only once the research is part of your workflow.
