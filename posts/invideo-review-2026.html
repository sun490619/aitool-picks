What is InVideo?

InVideo is an online video creation platform aimed at people who need a finished video without learning Premiere. You can start from one of 5,000+ templates or hand the AI a prompt or a URL, and it assembles a script, matches stock footage, adds a voiceover and captions, then drops you into a timeline to tweak. It is not a frame-by-frame compositor — it is a fast path from idea to publishable clip for social posts, ads, and explainers.

The product sits in a crowded middle ground: more guided than a raw editor like Premiere or DaVinci, but more flexible than fully automated generators that hand you a single locked output. Where it earns its keep is the moment you have a message but no footage, no script, and fifteen minutes. Instead of staring at a blank canvas, you start from a structure that already moves, then shape it. For teams that ship content on a calendar, that difference compounds week after week.

Who should use InVideo?

  • Social media marketers — churning out short promos and reels on a weekly cadence without a production team behind them.
  • Solo creators and small teams — no video editor on staff, but a constant need for content that looks credible.
  • Agencies — spinning up client videos from templates without rebuilding every project from a blank timeline.
  • Educators and course builders — turning lesson notes into narrated explainers without recording themselves on camera.

Key features

1. AI text-to-video

Give it a topic or paste an article and InVideo drafts a script, selects relevant stock clips, and lays them on a timeline with voiceover and subtitles. It is the fastest way to turn a written idea into a 60-second video without opening an editor from scratch.

2. Template library (5,000+)

Categories span ads, intros, YouTube, slideshows and social formats. Each template brings preset motion, layout and branding so a non-designer can look professional in minutes.

3. Built-in voiceover and subtitles

Generate a natural voiceover in many languages and auto-caption it. That alone saves the tedious export-and-subtitle loop most editors dread, and helps reach non-English audiences without a second tool. You can pick the voice, adjust pacing, and regenerate a line if the tone feels off — useful when the same script needs to ship in three languages for different regions.

4. Brand kit

Set colors, logos and fonts once and apply them across every video. For a team, that keeps output on-brand without re-checking each export. It also cuts the "why does this clip look different from last week's" drift that happens when everyone builds from scratch.

5. Stock media

Millions of stock clips and images are wired in, so you are not hunting across marketplaces for B-roll. Higher tiers raise the resolution and remove the watermark.

6. Collaboration and export

Share a project, leave comments, and export in 1080p or higher depending on plan. Direct publishing to YouTube and social platforms is supported.

!

Worth knowing before you start

The AI output is a strong starting point, not a finished film. The default clips and pacing can feel generic, so plan to swap a few shots and tighten the script for something that reads as yours.

A day with InVideo

You need a 45-second promo by end of day. You paste your blog announcement into InVideo, pick a template, and within minutes you have a drafted video with voiceover and captions. You swap two clips, adjust the brand color, shorten the intro, and export in 1080p. What would have been a two-hour edit becomes a 20-minute pass — leaving the afternoon for the actual work of publishing and promotion.

The value isn't the AI writing your script — it is removing the blank-timeline dread. You start from something on screen, then make it yours, instead of building from zero.

Frequently asked questions

Can InVideo turn text into a full video?

Yes. InVideo's AI mode takes a prompt or a pasted article and generates a script, picks stock footage, adds voiceover and subtitles, then lets you edit the result on a timeline.

Is there a free plan?

Yes. InVideo offers a free plan with watermarks and limited exports; paid plans remove the watermark and raise export limits and resolution.

Do I need editing experience to use InVideo?

No. The template library and AI assistant handle most of the layout, but comfortable editing helps you get a more original result than the default output.

Does InVideo support multiple languages?

Yes. You can generate voiceovers and subtitles in many languages, which helps localize videos for non-English audiences without a separate tool.

The gap between draft and finished

The AI draft is close, not done. In our test, the first pass used stock that didn't quite match the script and a pacing that felt busy at 45 seconds. Five minutes of swapping clips and trimming the intro made it read as a real promo. The lesson: treat the generated video as a scaffold, not a final cut, and you'll ship faster than starting blank.

Templates are a shortcut, not a crutch

With 5,000+ templates, the risk is sameness — many creators pick the same popular layouts and viewers start recognizing the look. The fix is small: change the color block, swap the hero clip, and write your own hook in the first line. Templates remove the layout work so you can spend that effort on the part that actually earns the click.
