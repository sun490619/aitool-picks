The Shortlist

If you just want the fast answer, here's where each tool fits:

  • Surfer SEO — best all-round content optimizer if you write and edit yourself ($89/mo).
  • Frase — best-value all-in-one (research + write + optimize) for solo creators ($49/mo).
  • NeuronWriter — cheapest serious optimizer with strong NLP scoring ($23/mo).
  • MarketMuse — best for large sites that need content inventory and topic modeling ($149/mo).
  • Scalenut — best if you want AI to draft ~90% of the article for you ($59/mo).
  • Originality.ai — best AI-content detector for verifying human-written work ($14.95/mo).

1. Surfer SEO — The Optimization Standard

From $89/mo (Essential) · ★★★★☆

Surfer SEO is the benchmark most other content optimizers are measured against. Its Content Editor scores your draft against the top-ranking pages for a keyword, then tells you exactly which terms, headings, and length to add to close the gap. The SERP Analyzer breaks down why competitors rank, and the Content Planner maps out a whole cluster of articles from a single seed keyword.

Best for: Content teams and bloggers who already write their own drafts and want data-driven guidance to push existing posts higher. The Google Docs and WordPress integrations let you optimize without leaving your editor.

Worth noting: Surfer tells you what to include, not how to write it — pair it with a writing tool if you want the drafting done for you. Newer AI features (AI Tracker, Auto-Optimize) add automation, but the core value is still its scoring engine.

2. Frase — Best All-in-One Value

From $49/mo · ★★★★☆

Frase compresses the entire content workflow — research, outline, draft, and optimize — into one tab. You paste a target keyword, and Frase pulls a SERP brief, extracts common headers and sources, and generates an AI draft you can edit inline. The Content Optimizer then scores your finished piece against the same SERP data.

Best for: Solo creators and small teams who want one subscription instead of three. At $49/mo it undercuts Surfer while covering the same ground, with the bonus of built-in drafting.

3. NeuronWriter — Cheapest Serious Optimizer

From $23/mo · ★★★★☆

NeuronWriter takes a semantic, Google-ranking-factors approach: it analyzes the top results for your query, builds a content plan from entities and NLP terms, and gives you a live optimization score as you write. The recommendations tend to be more "topic coverage" focused than Surfer's, which many writers find more natural.

Best for: Budget-conscious creators who still want professional-grade optimization. At $23/mo the entry plan is the lowest of the group, and it includes enough credits for several articles a month.

4. MarketMuse — Best for Large Content Operations

From $149/mo · ★★★☆☆

MarketMuse is the enterprise veteran. Instead of optimizing one page at a time, it models your whole site as a topic graph, scores content against "content authority," and flags gaps where you're weak. Its personalized difficulty score is more realistic than raw keyword difficulty because it accounts for your existing authority on a topic.

Best for: Established sites and content teams managing hundreds of pages who need inventory, planning, and a defensible content strategy — not just per-article tuning. The price reflects that positioning; it's overkill for a solo blog.

5. Scalenut — AI-First Content Creation

From $59/mo (Starter; promo $24/mo) · ★★★★☆

Scalenut positions itself as an "AI-first" SEO platform — meaning the AI doesn't just guide you, it generates the content. The Cruise Mode feature produces a complete 2000+ word article with SERP research, NLP optimization, and an outline in about 5 minutes.

Best for: Content teams that want AI to do the heavy lifting. If you prefer editing over writing from scratch, Scalenut's workflow is the most "hands-off" of the five.

6. Originality.ai — Best for AI Content Detection

From $14.95/mo · ★★★★★

Originality.ai is the most accurate AI detector on the market, built specifically for publishers and agencies who need to verify human-written content. It detects GPT-4, Claude, and other LLMs with 99%+ accuracy, plus checks for plagiarism and fact-checking.

Standout feature: Team management with scan history, API access for bulk checking, and the only detector that reliably catches paraphrased AI content.

Who it's for: Publishers, agencies, editors, and anyone buying content who needs to verify it's human-written.

Affiliate tip: 25% recurring for 12 months. At $14.95/mo, a single referral earns ~$3.75/month — low ticket but high volume potential with AI content explosion.

Our Recommendation

If you're a solo creator on a budget: Frase ($49/mo) or NeuronWriter ($23/mo). Both give you professional-grade optimization without the enterprise price tag.

If you run an agency or content team: Surfer SEO ($99/mo). The AI Tracker, Content Score, and Google Docs integration are worth the premium when content is your business.

If you want the AI to do 90% of the work: Scalenut ($59/mo; often discounted to $24/mo). Cruise Mode is genuinely impressive for churning out first drafts.
