What is eDesk?

eDesk is a customer-support platform designed for e-commerce. It pulls every conversation from your marketplaces, email and social channels into one shared inbox, then layers AI on top to help you reply faster and in the buyer's own language. It is not a generic ticketing tool retrofitted for retail — marketplaces and orders are first-class citizens here. Connect an Amazon or Shopify account and the inbox fills with real customer threads, each one already linked to the order behind it.

Who should use eDesk?

  • Sellers on 2+ channels — anyone splitting attention between Amazon, Shopify, eBay, Walmart or Etsy.
  • Small support teams — shared inbox, assignment and notes keep everyone on the same page.
  • Solo founders — drowning in "where is my order" and refund requests that all sound the same.

Key features

1. One unified inbox

Amazon, eBay, Shopify, Walmart, Etsy, Trustpilot, email, live chat and social in a single view. Assign, tag, snooze and filter without tab-hopping. This alone removes most of the daily friction for multi-channel sellers.

2. AI reply suggestions

For each message, eDesk drafts a reply using the conversation and the buyer's order context. You edit and send in seconds instead of typing from scratch. The suggestions get better as it learns your tone and common scenarios.

3. AI summaries and sentiment

Long, messy threads get summarized into a few bullet points. Sentiment analysis flags angry or at-risk buyers so they rise to the top of the queue — exactly the ones you can't afford to leave waiting.

4. Order-centric tickets

The order history, tracking status and refund state sit right inside the conversation. You can issue a refund or send tracking without leaving the inbox, which cuts the usual back-and-forth with another dashboard.

5. Multilingual by default

eDesk translates the buyer's message and drafts your reply in their language. For sellers serving a global customer base, that means one inbox handles German, Spanish, French and Japanese buyers without a separate translation tool.

6. Macros, saved replies and automations

One-click answers for "delivered", "out of stock" and "return policy". Rules auto-route by marketplace or topic and auto-assign to the right agent, so repetitive work disappears before a human even sees it.

7. Reporting

Dashboards show response times, backlog, CSAT and agent workload. You can see whether support is actually getting faster week over week — useful whether you're a team of one or ten.

A day with eDesk

Open eDesk at 9am. 42 new messages across three marketplaces. eDesk has already summarized the long ones and suggested replies for the routine ones. A German buyer wrote in German — you read the English translation, approve a drafted reply, and it goes out in German. A late shipment gets a one-click refund from the order panel. Fifteen "item delivered" questions get closed in a batch with a saved reply. By 9:40 the queue is clear, and you go back to actually growing the store.

Pricing

eDesk starts with a free plan so you can connect a marketplace and try the inbox with no commitment. Paid plans scale with the number of agents and message volume, and unlock more AI features and deeper reporting. Plans change, so check the current pricing on the site before you decide.

Frequently asked questions

Does eDesk connect to Amazon and Shopify?

Yes. eDesk has native integrations for Amazon, eBay, Shopify, Walmart, Etsy and more, plus email, live chat and social channels, all in one inbox.

Can eDesk reply in the buyer's language?

Yes. Its AI translation drafts and translates replies so a seller can serve buyers in multiple languages from a single inbox without switching tools.

Is there a free plan?

Yes. eDesk offers a free plan to start; paid plans add more agents, higher message volume, and advanced AI features.

Do I need to code to use eDesk?

No. Connect your marketplace and email accounts and the inbox populates automatically. No coding is required for daily support work.
