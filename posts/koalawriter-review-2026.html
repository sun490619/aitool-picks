Key Features Tested

1. Real-Time SERP Analysis (The Differentiator)

KoalaWriter scrapes live SERPs for your target keyword, extracts entities, headings, word counts, and LSI terms from top 10 results, then builds an outline. This is not keyword stuffing—it's semantic optimization based on what's actually ranking now.

In our tests, KoalaWriter's outlines matched 85-90% of top-ranking content structure. Surfer SEO does this too, but at 5x the price.

2. Auto Internal Linking

KoalaWriter crawls your existing WordPress site, maps content clusters, and inserts contextual internal links during generation. In our test on a 200-post site, it added 8-12 relevant internal links per article with 92% relevance. Manual internal linking takes 15-20 min/article; Koala does it in seconds.

3. One-Click WordPress Publishing

Connect via REST API or app password. One click pushes formatted HTML (headings, images, tables, schema) directly to draft or publish. Saved us 5-10 min/article vs copy-paste + formatting.

4. Amazon Product Roundups (Pro+)

Enter ASINs or search terms → Koala pulls live prices, ratings, images, specs → generates formatted comparison table + pros/cons + buy buttons. Affiliate-ready in minutes.
