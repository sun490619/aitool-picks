What is Mockey.ai?

Mockey.ai is a free online mockup generator with 27,000+ templates across 60+ categories — apparel, accessories, tech, packaging, posters, and more. You pick a template, drop in your design, and download a watermark-free JPG. It also bundles AI tools: background removal, background blur, and an "AI photoshoot" that generates realistic product visuals without a camera.

Key features we tested

We signed up, uploaded a sample logo, and pushed it through templates and AI tools to see what survives contact with a real listing.

Huge mockup library

The 27,000+ templates cover everything from T-shirts and hoodies to iPhone cases and packaging. For print-on-demand sellers this alone replaces hours of hunting for the right blank. You upload your artwork and it snaps into place.

Custom & multi-shot mockups

"Custom Mockup" turns any product photo into an editable template. "Multi Shot" generates front, back, and side views so a listing looks complete. "Mockup Collection" keeps a series on-brand.

AI background remover & blur

The background remover cleanly cuts out products; the blur tool softens busy backdrops. Both are one-click and good enough for marketplace listings.

AI photoshoot

Instead of renting a studio, the AI photoshoot produces realistic product scenes from a simple input. For sellers testing ad creative, this is a fast way to spin up variations.

Animation & video

There is an "Animate" option to turn a static mockup into a short clip, plus 5-second video mockups for social reels.

Pricing

Mockey.ai leads with a free plan: 27,000+ templates, uploads, and watermark-free JPG downloads — limited to a few downloads per day, with some features restricted. An Enterprise tier offers custom on-demand templates but pricing is quoted on request rather than listed publicly. For most solo sellers the free plan covers everyday needs.

Who should use Mockey.ai?

E-commerce and print-on-demand sellers who need volume and speed will get the most value. Social media managers testing creative, and solo founders without a designer, will also find it handy. If you need fully bespoke, art-directed visuals, pair it with a human designer.

For sellers who live and die by product visuals, it is an easy win — use the free plan daily and only talk to sales if you outgrow it. The AI photoshoot is the standout: realistic scenes without a camera, perfect for spinning up ad variations.

Frequently asked questions

Is Mockey.ai really free?

Yes. The free plan includes 27,000+ templates, uploads, and watermark-free JPG downloads, capped at a few downloads per day with some features restricted. An Enterprise tier is quote-only. For most solo sellers the free plan covers everyday needs.

Do the downloads have a watermark?

No. Even on the free plan, exports are watermark-free JPGs. That is unusually generous at this scale and is the main reason we keep it in our toolkit.

What does the AI photoshoot actually do?

Instead of renting a studio, it generates realistic product scenes from a simple input. For sellers testing ad creative it is a fast way to spin up variations without a camera or a designer.

Is the free plan enough for a real store?

For everyday listings, yes. The daily download cap can stall large batch jobs, so high-volume sellers may hit the limit. If you need unlimited throughput or custom on-demand templates, you'd talk to sales about Enterprise.

Template library and how well it fits your brand

Mockey's value is the template library: you pick a product category, drop in your design or image, and it drops the mockup into a realistic scene without you touching Photoshop. In our testing the apparel, packaging, and device frames were the strongest, with believable lighting and perspective that read as shot, not composited.

The catch is brand fit. Templates are generic scenes, so if your brand has a specific aesthetic, you'll still tweak backgrounds or recolor to match. We found the fastest path was to pick the closest template, then adjust the backdrop and add a consistent color grade so the set feels like one campaign. It won't replace a dedicated studio shoot for hero images, but for volume marketplace and social visuals it's a genuine time saver.

Worth checking: some premium scenes sit behind higher plans, so the free tier is enough to learn but not to ship a full catalog.

Export formats and where the mockups actually get used

Mockups are only useful if they land where the work happens. Mockey exports at resolutions suitable for e-commerce thumbnails and social posts, and we pushed results straight into listing images and ad creative without a re-export step. For most storefront needs that's enough; for print or large banner you'll want to confirm the pixel dimensions before committing.

Practically, the biggest win was speed to variant: one product, ten scenes, ten listings in an afternoon instead of a photo session. The limitation is control, you're bound by the template's framing, so highly art-directed shots still need a human designer. Use Mockey to fill the long tail of placeholder and A/B visuals, and reserve the hero slot for crafted assets.

We treat it as a scaled production layer, not a replacement for the one image that defines the brand.
