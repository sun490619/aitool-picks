Frequently asked questions

Do Submagic's captions actually boost watch time?

On our test clips, animated captions with emoji highlights measurably improved retention on short-form — viewers watch muted, and dynamic captions keep attention. The effect is real but plateaus; content still decides.

What does Submagic do?

Auto-generates styled captions, emoji highlights, b-roll suggestions, and vertical resizing for short-form clips. It's a finishing tool for TikTok, Reels, and Shorts, not a full editor.

Is Submagic worth it vs CapCut?

CapCut does more for free but takes longer per clip. Submagic wins on speed and caption polish at volume — worth it if you publish shorts daily.
