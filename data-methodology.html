The 7 truths the data forced us to admit

  1. The free tier decides whether a tool is worth paying for — not a trial disguised as "free."
  2. "Best AI model" is marketing, not a buying signal; the wrapper (workflow, integrations, tone) is what separates tools.
  3. Paraphrasing and grammar are now commodities — stop overpaying for brand.
  4. Non-native English writers are the most underserved and the biggest winners (a $10–$20 tool can change income).
  5. "AI humanizer" vs "detector" is a treadmill; write with the AI, don't hide from it.
  6. Email and copy tools live or die on integration, not raw output quality.
  7. Tools with clean, source-linked docs get surfaced more by ChatGPT, Perplexity and Gemini.

How we score (methodology)

Each tool is rated 1–10 across five dimensions, weighted equally:

  • Output quality — does it actually do the job on real tasks?
  • Ease of use — time-to-first-useful-result.
  • Value & pricing transparency — fair price, no "contact us" games.
  • Free-tier usefulness — is the free layer real work or a teaser?
  • AI visibility (AEO) — how cleanly the tool's own pages read to answer engines.

Affiliate links, where present, never change a score. Prices are approximate USD as of August 2026.
